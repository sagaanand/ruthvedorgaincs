Our Journey

From seed to shelf, our story unfolds

2010

The Beginning

Founded with a small family farm and a vision to bring authentic organic products to every Indian household. Our first product was traditional Desi Ghee made using the Bilona method.

2013

First Expansion

Introduced cold-pressed oils and natural honey to our product line. Expanded our farmer network to include 50+ organic farmers across Karnataka.

2016

New Facility

Established our own processing facility in Bangalore with traditional wood-press technology to ensure chemical-free extraction while preserving nutrients.

2019

National Recognition

Awarded "Best Organic Brand" by the National Organic Association. Expanded our distribution to 5 states across India.

2023

Present Day

Serving over 50,000 satisfied customers nationwide with 15+ organic products, while maintaining our commitment to traditional methods and sustainable practices.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Authenticity

We preserve traditional methods like Bilona ghee-making and wood-pressed oil extraction to deliver products as nature intended.

Purity

No chemicals, no additives, no compromises. Every product is certified organic and free from artificial processing.

Community

We partner with local farmers, ensuring fair trade practices and supporting sustainable agriculture across India.

Sustainability

From eco-friendly packaging to waste reduction in production, we minimize our environmental footprint at every step.

Wellness

Our products are designed to nourish your body and enhance your wellbeing, just as traditional Indian foods have for centuries.

Excellence

We maintain the highest quality standards through rigorous testing and continuous improvement in our processes.

Our Mission

To reconnect Indian families with the nourishing power of traditional foods by making authentic, chemical-free organic products accessible to everyone. We are committed to preserving ancient food wisdom while supporting sustainable farming practices that honor both people and the planet.

Every jar of Desi Ghee, every drop of cold-pressed oil, and every spoonful of honey we produce carries this mission forward.
